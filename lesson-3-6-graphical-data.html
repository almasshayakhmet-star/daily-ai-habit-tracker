💡 What is a graphical representation of data?

Chart

A chart, also called a graph, is a graphical representation of data. Charts have different formats such as bar chart, line chart, and pie chart.

Bar chart – Use a bar chart when you want to compare individual items.
(e.g. prices of different smartphones – Picture 3.6.1)
Line chart – Use a line chart when you want to show rate of change.
(e.g. change of temperature during the week – Picture 3.6.2)
Pie chart – Use a pie chart when you want to visualise a part-to-whole relationship in percentages.
(e.g. time spent for different activities – Pic. 3.6.3)

How to create a chart

To create a chart, do the following steps:

  1. Create a table on the range A1:E6 (Picture 3.6.4).
  2. Select the range A2:E6.
  3. Click on Insert → Chart.
  4. Set up / customise the chart data, select chart type, etc. on the chart editor toolbar.
